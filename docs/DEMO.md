# Three-minute demo script

**0:00–0:20 — frame the weekly decision.** Open CRE Activity Radar and say: “This is a public-signal research queue for a CRE market research analyst supporting a broker's weekly review. It does not predict a market outcome. It helps decide what deserves the next research step, then preserves the evidence for that decision.” Point to the fixed Jul 2025–Jun 2026 versus Jul 2024–Jun 2025 comparison.

**0:20–1:25 — begin with one Chicago finding.** Select Chicago and keep All types. Start with the first card under **Areas worth investigating**. Read its current/prior counts and absolute change aloud, then select it. The card is a concrete observed change in recorded Chicago permit activity, not an opportunity score.

Open the **Investigation Brief**. Walk through why it surfaced, the permit-type mix, active months and peak, repeated supplied addresses, and the largest individual reported estimates. Say: “This tells an analyst what to inspect next. Repeated addresses may be related filings; reported estimates are incomplete applicant fields, not investment totals.” Open one listed record and its publisher link to show that the brief resolves to source evidence.

**1:25–1:55 — make trust visible.** In the brief, show quality and limits: mapped source coordinates, missing reported costs, and the Chicago permit-issuance warning. Explain that issuance is not a construction start, completion, or unique project. Then show source health: accepted, rejected, duplicate, mapped, and unmapped accounting. The map is supporting H3 context, not a neighborhood or a commercial signal.

**1:55–2:30 — demonstrate two-city extensibility without false comparability.** Switch to New York City. The same queue-to-brief-to-evidence workflow runs against NYC DOB NOW: Build while the brief retains the NYC source caveat. Change the permit-type filter and select a finding. Say: “Two markets demonstrate that the workflow survives heterogeneous schemas and source semantics. They are never ranked against each other, and NYC DOB NOW Build is not all NYC permit activity.”

**2:30–3:00 — show separate SBA context and close.** Show the SBA 504 panel. Say: “These are city-labelled borrower approvals, separate from permit areas. They never color a cell or change the queue because borrower city is not project geography and approval is not a disbursement or demand measure.” Close with: “The product delivers an auditable lead and the next verification steps. The analyst then checks property, ownership, leasing, zoning, and broker context before making a market claim.”

If the source-health panel reports a partial source, state that counts are a sample. The queue then orders current recorded activity only and does not make a growth claim.
